### [CheckPoint Student](https://ckpnt.com)

> **NFC-based student presence and attendance for school campuses.** Students tap in, teachers and administrators see who's where in real time.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td><b>Piloted on campus</b></td>
    <td>Built and piloted at La Jolla Country Day School with students, teachers, and administrators</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td><b>NAIS Annual Conference</b></td>
    <td>Exhibited with our own booth in Nashville, TN, presenting to thousands of educators</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td><b>Mobile apps</b></td>
    <td>Native iOS and Flutter apps for quick NFC check-ins, plus a campus kiosk app</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td><b>Teacher Chrome extension</b></td>
    <td>One-click attendance tools for teachers, right in the browser</td>
  </tr>

    <td><b>Admin dashboards</b></td>
    <td>Attendance tools, a student manager, and a senior-privileges tracker.</td>
  </tr>
